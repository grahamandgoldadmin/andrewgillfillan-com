What I've built at WKAR.

I arrived in June 2025 to a strong newsroom with a digital front door that was not yet doing it justice. Since then: two newsletters launched, a statewide politics brand built, the digital audience roughly doubled, and a freelance reporter system put in place to protect capacity after federal funding cuts.

Four case studies, each one a product that still ships every week without me in the room.

The operating change

Four things I changed about how the newsroom runs

Audience strategy

Analytics moved out of the management report and onto the newsroom floor. Everyone sees what the audience reads, what it ignores, and what we do about it.

Editorial standards

Depth in every story, every day. In-depth reporting became a standing commitment rather than an occasional special project.

Talent & capacity

Full-time staff plus a freelance reporter system implemented on arrival, which protected coverage capacity when federal funding was cut.

Product & distribution

Two newsletters and a politics destination gave the reporting somewhere to live and a reason to return, and gave the newsroom a repeatable publishing system.
