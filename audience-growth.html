How, not luck

The method

01

Show the team the numbers

Analytics stopped being a management document. Everyone sees what the audience actually reads, and what it ignores.

02

Depth in every story

I challenged the newsroom to go past the headline every day, and launched in-depth reporting as a standing commitment, not a special project.

03

Own the channels

Two newsletters and a politics destination gave the work somewhere to live and a reason to return. Distribution is editorial work.

04

Put names on it

Bylines, faces and beats moved to the front. Audiences follow people more reliably than they follow institutions.
